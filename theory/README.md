# Lunar Flower — Theory index

## AI seed: expert-system contract

The **Lunar Flower AI Seed** is published in the central Ethical & Intellectual Theory repository:

[Read: Lunar Flower AI Seed — Expert-System Contract](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/blue-prints/LUNAR_FLOWER_AI_SEED_EXPERT_SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md)

**Core statement:** place AI under an expert-system contract in an orchestration role; prefer rule-governed procedures; make generative synthetic decision-making a last resort; build the tools and governing structure from the ground up.

**First proposed gates:** [Production Lock](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/blue-prints/LUNAR_FLOWER_AI_SEED_EXPERT_SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md#gate-1--production-lock), [Reiteration](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/blue-prints/LUNAR_FLOWER_AI_SEED_EXPERT_SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md#gate-2--reiteration), and [Hand-off](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/blue-prints/LUNAR_FLOWER_AI_SEED_EXPERT_SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md#gate-3--hand-off). These definitions are generic design proposals; none claims an operating control.

**Gate 4 — Informed Operator Validation:** [Read the proposed gate](GATE_4_INFORMED_OPERATOR_VALIDATION.md). Acknowledgment alone does not establish understanding, consent, or authorization. When explicit human validation is required, the response must demonstrate relevant understanding; dangerous or sensitive actions require heightened scrutiny. Insufficient validation preserves the production lock. This is a proposed contract, not an active enforcement mechanism.

**Status:** theoretical proposal, not an activated AI control system and not a capability claim about Twin Lanterns or ReShade shader builds.
