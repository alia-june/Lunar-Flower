## AI seed: expert-system contract

The **Lunar Flower AI Seed** is published in the central Ethical & Intellectual Theory repository:

[Read: Lunar Flower AI Seed — Expert-System Contract](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/blue-prints/LUNAR_FLOWER_AI_SEED_EXPERT_SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md)

**Core statement:** place AI under an expert-system contract in an orchestration role; prefer rule-governed procedures; make generative synthetic decision-making a last resort; build the tools and governing structure from the ground up.

**Standing gates (one canonical document):** [Production Lock, Reiteration, Hand-off, Informed Operator Validation, and Consequence-Scaled Challenge–Response](https://github.com/alia-june/ethical-and-intellectual-theory/blob/main/blue-prints/LUNAR_FLOWER_AI_SEED_EXPERT_SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md#standing-expert-system-gates--generic-proposed-contracts). All five belong to the same proposed expert-system contract. Gate 4 requires contextual understanding where explicit human validation is required; Gate 5 provides optional consequence-scaled scrutiny through independent risk, challenge, verification, stability, and Gate 1 review controls. No documentation here activates an operating control.

**Status:** theoretical proposal, not an activated AI control system and not a capability claim about Twin Lanterns or ReShade shader builds.
